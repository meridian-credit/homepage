2026-09-30 14:20 — Desktop mega-menu stutter, measured in Chromium (M2 Max, 1440@2x): the main thread was never the problem (60 fps, ~2 ms layout per open); two things were. (1) Choreography: a single pane was re-mounted per open and slid to its trigger with a 0.24 s `margin-left` transition (it flew in from the left edge on first open and glided 270 px on SERVICE↔BLOG); on close the links vanished and, on dark-hero pages, the empty panel collapsed as a navy slab; the sheet snapped white while the text took 0.28 s. (2) The header's SVG refraction (`glass-filter.tsx`: two `feImage` maps) is re-drawn on every frame in which something inside the header moves: uncapped throughput while the menu animates was ~180 fps with it versus ~1,850 without, so 120 Hz screens and weaker GPUs drop frames. With the panel open the sheet is 0.8 white and the refraction barely shows (mean diff 0.3/255), so it is off while the menu is open and comes back 0.3 s after close (delayed discrete transition); after the fix, uncapped throughput is ~1,570 fps. Not changed, but noted: the header's schedule cube (`sc-roll`, infinite) keeps the header repainting ~60 fps on every page ≥1360 px, and each of its roll frames pays the same refraction cost (~2–3 ms of compositor time), an idle CPU/battery cost rather than stutter.
